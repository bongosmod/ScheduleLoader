# Please Read 👋

Thanks for downloading **Schedule Loader**! Here's everything you need to get going.

## "Windows protected your PC"?

The first time you open `ScheduleLoader.exe`, Windows may show a blue box saying **"Windows protected your PC"**.

That's Windows SmartScreen. It shows up for apps that aren't signed with a paid certificate, which is common for small, free projects like this one. It doesn't mean anything was found in the file.

To open Schedule Loader anyway:

1. Click **More info**.
2. Click **Run anyway**.

You only need to do this once.

You can read every line of Schedule Loader's code on GitHub: https://github.com/bongosmod/ScheduleLoader

## What you need

- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
- **Schedule I** on Steam, the main version (not the "alternate" beta branch)

## Getting started

1. Close Schedule I if it's running.
2. Open `ScheduleLoader.exe` and click **Set up**. It installs everything mods need (MelonLoader, S1API and S1MAPI) for you. The first time, it starts the game once to prepare it, which can take a few minutes.
3. Open the **Browser** tab and install the mods you want.
4. Press **Launch** to play with your mods.

Mods update themselves: whenever a new version is out, Schedule Loader picks it up the next time you open it.

## Changed your mind?

**Uninstall** in Schedule Loader removes everything it installed, mods included. Your saves are never touched.

## Something not working?

Schedule Loader keeps a log here:
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ScheduleLoader\ScheduleLoader.log`

and the game's mod log is here:
`<your Schedule I folder>\MelonLoader\Latest.log`

Please include them when you report a problem.
